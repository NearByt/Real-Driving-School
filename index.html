Install on iPhone / iPad

To install Real Driving School Portal on your iOS device:

1. Tap the Share icon at the bottom of Safari.
2. Scroll down and select 'Add to Home Screen' .
3. Tap 'Add' in the top-right corner.

The RDS Portal icon will now appear on your iPhone home screen for instant fullscreen app experience!
